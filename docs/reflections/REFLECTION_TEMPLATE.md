## 1. What did you understand from the requirement?
Explain the requirement in your own words.

Focus on:
- what problem needed to be solved;
- what behaviour was expected;
- important constraints you identified.

Do not copy the Backlog description.

## 2. What did you implement?
Briefly describe the work you completed.

Mention:
- major functionality added or changed;
- important files or components created or modified;
- how your implementation satisfies the requirement.

## 3. What important engineering decisions did you make?
For each important decision, explain:
- what you decided;
- why you chose that approach;
- alternatives you considered, if any;
- important trade-offs.

For a lasting project decision, write the full explanation once in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and link to its decision ID or heading here. Briefly describe how it applies to this backlog. Keep backlog-specific choices here; no new decision entry is required if none was introduced.

## 4. What problems or uncertainties did you encounter?
Describe significant issues such as unclear requirements, implementation difficulty, unexpected behaviour, integration problems, failing tests, data issues, or design uncertainty.

If you did not encounter a significant problem, state that briefly rather than inventing one.

## 5. How did you resolve them?
Explain:
- what you investigated;
- what you tried;
- what worked or did not work;
- why you selected the final solution.

If Section 4 contains no significant problem, state that this section is not applicable.

## 6. How did you verify your work?
Describe how you established that the implementation works.

Mention relevant:
- test cases;
- automated tests;
- manual verification;
- edge cases;
- validation queries;
- sample inputs and outputs.

Do not simply write "tested successfully."

## 7. What remains incomplete, limited, or worth improving?
Mention any known limitation, technical debt, unsupported case, incomplete work, assumption, or improvement opportunity.

If none are known, state that explicitly. Do not invent limitations merely to complete this section.

## 8. What resources or assistance did you use?
Record significant external assistance such as official documentation, technical articles, Stack Overflow or similar resources, AI assistants, IDE coding assistants, or help from another person.

For AI-assisted work, briefly state:
- what the AI helped you with;
- what output or suggestion you used;
- how you independently verified the relevant work.

## 9. Evidence
List relevant evidence included in the repository.

Example:
- `evidence/<EXACT_ASSIGNED_BACKLOG_CODE>/order-created.png` — successful order creation
- `evidence/<EXACT_ASSIGNED_BACKLOG_CODE>/invalid-stock.png` — insufficient-stock validation
- `tests/...` — automated verification

Only include evidence relevant to the assigned Backlog. If no separate evidence was required, state that here.

## 10. Final Reflection
In a few sentences, state:
- the most important thing you learned from this work;
- what you would approach differently if doing similar work again.

Keep this concise and specific to the work completed.
